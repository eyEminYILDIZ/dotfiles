# Obsidian

Obsidian is a knowledge management tool that helps you organize and manage your notes efficiently. It uses a local folder of plain text Markdown files as a database, allowing you to create a network of linked notes.

## Features

- **Markdown Support**: Write and edit notes using Markdown syntax.
- **Bidirectional Linking**: Easily create links between notes to build a knowledge graph.
- **Graph View**: Visualize the connections between your notes in a graph format.
- **Plugins and Themes**: Extend functionality and customize the appearance of Obsidian with community plugins and themes.
- **Local Storage**: Keep your notes stored locally on your device for privacy and control.
- **Search and Organization**: Quickly search and organize your notes using tags, folders, and backlinks.
- **Cross-Platform**: Available on Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, and Android, allowing you to access your notes from any device.
- **Community and Support**: Benefit from a large community of users and extensive documentation for troubleshooting and learning new features.
